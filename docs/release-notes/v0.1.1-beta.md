Wonderpark 0.1.1-beta: fixes for the first beta of Wonderpark, an unofficial PokéPark 2 recomp. Use this instead of 0.1.0-beta.

> Wonderpark is an unofficial, non-commercial fan project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Nintendo, The Pokémon Company, Creatures Inc. or Game Freak. **This download contains no game files.** You need your own disc image of PokéPark 2: Wonders Beyond (US, S2LE01); the launcher builds the game from it on your PC. **Never share your built Wonderpark folder**: once built, it contains the game.

## Fixed since 0.1.0-beta

- **Frame rate:** changing it in F1 (or the game's Options page) now takes effect at once. Before, the old rate stayed until the next start.
- **Show FPS:** a clean badge in the corner (the number, "FPS" and a coloured dot) instead of blocky digits. Green means you're getting the frame rate you chose, or your screen's refresh rate if that's lower.
- **Sound:** no more chopped sound after loading a save. Fast-forward (Tab, or the left trigger when nothing is bound to it) is now off by default: it muted the sound while held, which sounded like choppy audio. Turn it on in F1 > Gameplay if you want it.
- **Much quicker first build:** about 10 minutes instead of 20-30. The launcher then makes a fully optimised build in the background while it's open and you're not playing (it stops whenever you play and carries on later) and uses it from then on.

Already on 0.1.0-beta? Unzip this into a new folder, run its launcher and build again. Your saves and settings carry over.

## Install

1. Install [Python 3](https://www.python.org/) (3.10 or newer) and the [Visual Studio Build Tools](https://visualstudio.microsoft.com/downloads/) with "Desktop development with C++" and "C++ Clang tools for Windows". For a plain `.iso`, `.wbfs` or `.rvz` image you also need [decomp-toolkit](https://github.com/encounter/decomp-toolkit) (`dtk.exe`) on your PATH; NKit images don't need it.
2. Unzip `Wonderpark-0.1.1-beta.zip` into a folder of its own (about 2 GB free needed; 2.7 GB more for the optional HD Pack). Avoid OneDrive and other synced folders.
3. Run `WonderparkLauncher.exe`, click the logo, **Place ISO**, **Build** (about 10 minutes on a recent 8-core PC), then **Play**.

## What's in it

- The game, recompiled to run natively on Windows: 60 FPS at the game's own speed (120 / 144 / 240 with blended frames), 2x-6x resolution with anti-aliasing, widescreen and ultrawide.
- Keyboard, mouse and controllers; no motion controls needed. Every action can be rebound.
- **F1** in game: every setting, the Friend Book, the Journal, the Jukebox (the game's music from your disc) and the Gallery.
- The launcher's **Extras**: mods from a .zip with on/off switches; **Saves** (back up, restore, export, import from Dolphin, a backup before every Play); **HD Pack** (the game's textures upscaled 4x on your PC; it downloads its upscaler once, about 14 MB: [4xNomosUni_span_multijpg](https://github.com/Phhofm/models/releases/tag/4xNomosUni_span_multijpg) by Philip Hofmann, CC BY 4.0, run by ONNX Runtime, MIT).
- Japanese Pokémon voices, from your own Japanese disc.

## Known issues

- A full playthrough hasn't been done yet: the story has been played through the first areas and all four attractions. Please report where things go wrong.
- Dance Inferno works only partly without motion controls (the shake keys dance whatever move is due).
- Local multiplayer (2-4 players) is in progress and untested for a full round.
- Skipping movies doesn't work yet.
- "Coming soon": some Journal stats (jumps, Thunderbolts, photos, distance, best scores), the Gallery's 3D view, the online mod list.
- The HD Pack leaves 5 title-screen pictures as they are.
- Only the US disc (S2LE01) is supported, on Windows 10/11 64-bit with OpenGL 3.3.

Found a bug? Open an issue; the template asks for what we need (your log, your settings, what you did). Don't attach game files.
