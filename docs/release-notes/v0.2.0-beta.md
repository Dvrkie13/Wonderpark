Wonderpark 0.2.0-beta: the first release of Wonderpark, an unofficial PokéPark 2 recomp for Windows.

> Wonderpark is an unofficial, non-commercial fan project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Nintendo, The Pokémon Company, Creatures Inc. or Game Freak. **This download contains no game files.** You need your own disc image of PokéPark 2: Wonders Beyond (US, S2LE01); the launcher builds the game from it on your PC. **Never share your built Wonderpark folder**: once built, it contains the game.

## Install

1. Unzip `Wonderpark-0.2.0-beta.zip` into a folder of its own (about 2 GB free needed; 2.7 GB more for the optional HD Pack). Avoid OneDrive and other synced folders. Nothing else needs installing: the first Build downloads its compiler.
2. Run `WonderparkLauncher.exe`, click the logo, **Place ISO**, **Build** (a few minutes on a recent PC), then **Play**. While the launcher is open and you're not playing, it makes a fully optimised build in the background and uses it from then on.

Already tried an earlier test build? Unzip this into a new folder and build again. Your saves and settings carry over.

## What's in it

- **The game, recompiled to run natively on Windows:** 60 FPS at the game's own speed (120 / 144 / 240 with blended frames), 2x-6x resolution with anti-aliasing, widescreen and ultrawide.
- **Keyboard, mouse and controllers**, no motion controls needed; every action can be rebound.
- **Runs on modest PCs:** graphics presets from Potato to Ultra, suggested by a speed test after the build; dynamic resolution; Battery saver.
- **F1 in game:** every setting, and four books:
  - **Friend Book:** who you've befriended, area by area.
  - **Journal:** play time, friends, jumps, Thunderbolts, milestones, and 33 **stickers** to earn (one slides in at the top of the screen when you get it).
  - **Jukebox:** the game's music from your disc; ♥ a song to have the launcher play it.
  - **Gallery:** your friends as their own 3D models, animated, with zoom, Save a picture, and **Play cry**.
- **The park's light:** Time of day can follow your PC's clock (golden evenings, blue nights); seasonal touches bring snow in December, leaves in late October and petals in spring.
- **Silly settings:** colour filters, a retro pixel look, paper Pokémon, Pokémon size, voice pitch, mirror world, victory confetti.
- **Accessibility and comfort:** gamma, a colour-blind filter, stick dead zones, a hold-or-toggle charge button, camera distance and smoothing, mono audio, Night mode, a key to hide the HUD.
- **The launcher:** music from songs you've unlocked in the game (only once your build is done), full controller support, and a **couch mode** for TVs (fullscreen with big button hints; automatic in Steam Big Picture and on Steam Deck, F11 any time). Its **Extras**: mods from a .zip with on/off switches; **Saves** (back up, restore, export, import from Dolphin, a backup before every Play); **HD Pack** (the game's textures upscaled 4x on your PC; it downloads its upscaler once, about 14 MB: [4xNomosUni_span_multijpg](https://github.com/Phhofm/models/releases/tag/4xNomosUni_span_multijpg) by Philip Hofmann, CC BY 4.0, run by ONNX Runtime, MIT).
- **Japanese Pokémon voices**, from your own Japanese disc.

## Known issues

- A full playthrough hasn't been done yet: the story has been played through the first areas and all four attractions. Please report where things go wrong.
- Dance Inferno works only partly without motion controls (the shake keys dance whatever move is due).
- Skipping movies doesn't work yet.
- "Coming soon": photos taken and distance in the Journal, attraction best scores and medals (and their stickers), some Silly settings (partner colours, Shiny park, Big heads, Moon jump, Rainbow dash), the online mod list.
- The game's own photos (the − button) haven't been checked yet.
- The HD Pack leaves 5 title-screen pictures as they are.
- Only the US disc (S2LE01) is supported, on Windows 10/11 64-bit with OpenGL 3.3.

Found a bug? Open an issue; the template asks for what we need (your log, your settings, what you did). Don't attach game files.
